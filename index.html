🧑 Adult Zone

1. Find the real starting point

She already knows uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and rhyming. The baseline checks letter sounds, short vowels, beginning/ending sounds, oral blending, oral segmenting, and CVC decoding.

Do not assume knowing a letter name means knowing its sound automatically.
✨ Kid Zone

2. Learn through play

Activities use treasure hunts, movement, magical characters, rainbow gems, princess castles, sound sorting, blending, and word building—without turning the session into a worksheet marathon.

Week 1 objective

Adjusted after the baseline.

Sound foundation

👂 Automatically produce a small set of consonant sounds.
🍎 Use short a and i.
🔎 Identify first sounds in spoken words.

Early word skills

🧩 Blend 2–3 spoken phonemes.
👏 Segment simple spoken words.
📖 Connect sounds to written CVC words only when ready.

🔎 Luna's Baseline Adventure

Adult-guided 5–10 minutes

Parent script

“Luna the Unicorn lost some rainbow crystals! She needs a Sound Detective. Every sound challenge helps Luna get a crystal back. Want to help?”

Give about 3 seconds per answer. Do not teach during the baseline. If she does not know one, say: “That's okay. Let's try another one.”

Recommended next focus

The app uses the baseline and Day 1 observations for a simple rule-based recommendation. It does not send child data to a server.

Mastery rule

Letter sound: about 4/5 without long hesitation.
Blending: several 2–3 phoneme combinations with little prompting.
Segmenting: most phonemes in simple CVC words.
CVC reading: about 4/5 taught-pattern words without picture guessing.
